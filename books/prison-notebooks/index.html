Critique · 1929–35

Prison Notebooks

Antonio Gramsci

Gramsci’s notebooks introduce hegemony, civil society and the war of position — how ruling ideas and consent sustain power, and how to contest them.

I · Reading notes

Main points

  • Hegemony combines coercion with consent built through civil society.
  • Intellectuals help organize social groups and their worldviews.
  • A war of position contests entrenched institutions and cultural authority over time.
II · Critical perspectives

Criticisms & debates

  • The fragmented, prison-constrained writing leaves key concepts open to conflicting interpretations.
  • Hegemony can become so broad that its explanatory limits and practical implications are unclear.

Editorial discussion points, not quotations or a substitute for reading the text.

Critique · 1899

Reform or Revolution?

Rosa Luxemburg

Luxemburg’s classic defense of revolutionary socialism against reformist revisionism within the SPD — trade union action alone cannot overturn capitalism.

I · Reading notes

Main points

  • Reforms and trade-union struggles matter, but cannot by themselves abolish capitalist property relations.
  • Luxemburg rejects the idea that capitalism’s contradictions have been permanently overcome.
  • Immediate struggles should remain connected to a socialist transformation.
II · Critical perspectives

Criticisms & debates

  • The argument may understate the lasting achievements possible through democratic reforms.
  • The institutional route from revolutionary struggle to a durable democratic socialism remains open.

Editorial discussion points, not quotations or a substitute for reading the text.

III

About This Library

This is a working library, not a monument. It is a place to read, annotate and think about the history and theory of the left — from the first utopias to the crises of the present.

The books are grouped by theme; the history is a walking timeline. Every entry is meant to be opened, argued with, and re-read. The tradition is alive so long as it is studied.
